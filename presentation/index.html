SAT PoC v1 — 17 sequence diagrams

Every flow of the pilot, in message order, with the branches that matter — including the work that happens before site commissioning. Companion to 04-design.md in the pack, which is the authoritative copy of these flows.

One site, one SAT, Cx2 and Cx3, no internet, no AI on the December path. The SAT format is ours, not the client's, and the project's instrument list supplies the steps. Where a diagram here and a flow table in 04-design.md disagree, the document wins and this copy is regenerated.

Before site commissioning

  1. S13

    Install, accounts and the tool token

    An empty Hub becomes a usable one; the format and the steps arrive in S15 and S16.

  2. S15

    Define our standard SAT format

    One master record, versioned by us, agreed before any site work.

  3. S16

    Instrument list to step list

    The project's IO or instrument list supplies the tags; our format supplies the layout.

  4. S17

    Scanned list to candidate, then a person commits

    Conditional and gated: it proposes, a person commits through the same import.

The pilot itself

  1. S1

    Sign in and carry a capability

    A local account behind a seam, a server-side session, and a restart that ends it.

  2. S2

    Open the app the next morning

    Session, grid, and what happened to yesterday's claim.

  3. S3

    Read a form and review its flags

    Columns, slots and sections come from the pinned schema version.

  4. S4

    Lease lifecycle

    Claim, race, renew, release, and an expiry nothing has to run.

  5. S5

    Capture to stored evidence

    Spool, reload, hash, deduplicate, one image per slot per revision.

  6. S6

    Write a step and survive the refusals

    A revision check, schema validation, and an evidence hash the Hub must already hold.

  7. S7

    Submit, flags, and form-level ready

    Saved is not finished; a person says when a step is done.

  8. S8

    Seal one form

    A person closes one form; a compile never seals anything.

  9. S9

    Amend, correct, re-seal, supersede

    A correction reopens named steps and issues the next edition of that form.

  10. S10

    The screenshot integration

    Trigger over HTTP through the Hub queue, the image back over HTTP, state derived on the clock.

  11. S11

    Compile and collect the bundle

    A job, a manifest, and a download a person uploads by hand.

  12. S12

    Daily summary, backup and restore

    Text a person copies, and two commands that run with no network.

  13. S14

    Site day

    Install, run one real SAT, seal, compile, upload, and a signer accepts.
